Tired of Having 80 Tabs Open in Chrome?

We built Manga Reader because of a real problem: keeping 80+ tabs open across multiple manga websites just to avoid losing track of chapters. One accidental browser restart or memory purge, and days of reading progress were gone.

Manga Reader replaces that chaos with a single elegant bookshelf. Paste any story URL or browse freely — the app automatically identifies the chapter, organizes it in your library, strips distracting popups, and saves your exact scroll position so you pick up right where you stopped.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Google Sign-In required?

Google Sign-In is required to securely identify your account and sync your library across devices using Google Cloud Firestore. This ensures you never lose your reading progress when upgrading or changing phones.

Does Manga Reader host manga files?

No. Manga Reader is strictly a client-side viewing utility, similar to a specialized web browser. It does not host, store, index, or distribute any copyrighted files. All media is fetched directly from public third-party URLs on your device.

Can I read chapters offline?

Yes. Chapters opened during your reading sessions are temporarily cached in your device's sandboxed storage, allowing you to reread them without an internet connection.

What sources can I view with Manga Reader?

Manga Reader is built and intended strictly for use on free web comic sites, open-access publications, and public domain materials without copyright restrictions. The sole legal and financial responsibility for verifying the copyright status and permission of third-party websites rests entirely with the end user. The developer does not host any media and bears zero legal or financial liability.
