Privacy Policy

How Manga Reader protects your privacy with secure Google Authentication, encrypted cloud sync, zero ad tracking, and client-side rendering.

Last Updated: November 12, 2026 • Effective Date: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Manga Reader ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Fays Arukattil, collects, manages, and safeguards user information across the mobile application and associated web documentation.

Summary: Private Client Utility & Sole User Responsibility

Manga Reader is a private, client-side browser and viewer utility intended exclusively for use on free web comics, public domain publications, and authorized digital sources without copyright restrictions. WE DO NOT HOST, STORE, ARCHIVE, OR DISTRIBUTE ANY MANGA, COMICS, OR MEDIA FILES. All browsing, image parsing, and reading occur directly on-device through your direct user input. The sole legal, regulatory, and financial responsibility for all content accessed rests entirely with the user; the Developer (Fays Arukattil) is not legally or financially liable or obligated in any manner whatsoever.

1. Mandatory Google Account & Data Collection

To provide a seamless experience where your reading library and exact chapter progress are preserved across your devices, Manga Reader requires users to authenticate with a valid Google Account. There is no unauthenticated guest mode in Manga Reader.

When you authenticate using Google Sign-In:

  • Google Identity Information: We receive your basic profile identity (name, email address, and unique user identifier / UID) via Google Firebase Authentication. We use this strictly to identify your account and authenticate your private storage space.
  • Reading Progress & Bookmarks: Your saved series, categories, reading history, and exact chapter/page scroll positions are synchronized to Google Cloud Firestore. This ensures that if you switch phones or reinstall the app, you immediately resume reading where you left off.
  • Database Isolation: Your library data is encrypted in transit and at rest on Google Cloud Firestore, secured by granular access rules granting access exclusively to your authenticated UID.

2. Local Device Storage & Cache

In addition to cloud backup, Manga Reader utilizes your device's sandboxed storage for high-performance reading:

  • High-Speed Local Cache: Images downloaded during active reading sessions are cached temporarily on your local device to eliminate duplicate network requests and conserve cellular bandwidth.
  • Cache Purging: You can purge cached comic strips at any time directly through the app settings or via your Android system application settings.
  • Offline Playback: Previously cached chapters can be read offline without an active internet connection.

3. Application Permissions

Manga Reader requests only the minimal system permissions required to operate:

  • INTERNET: Required to download images directly from the third-party websites you browse and to synchronize your library via Google Firebase.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Detects whether your device has an active network connection to smoothly serve cached pages when offline.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Keeps your screen awake while actively reading chapters so your display does not time out mid-page.

4. Third-Party Services & Network Requests

When utilizing Manga Reader:

  • Direct Web Navigation: Web requests for comic pages are dispatched directly from your mobile device to the third-party website or content delivery network hosting that content. We do not operate proxy servers, intermediary scrapers, or surveillance networks.
  • Google Firebase Services: We use Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore provided by Google LLC. Google's privacy practices are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Data Deletion & Account Rights

You retain absolute ownership and control over your data:

  • Account Sign-Out: You may sign out of your Google account at any time via the profile settings sheet.
  • Cloud Data Deletion: You may request permanent deletion of your account record and all associated Cloud Firestore library documents by sending an email with your registered Google account address to faysarukattil@gmail.com. All records will be expunged within 30 days.
  • Local Data Wipe: Clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app permanently purges all local cache and sandboxed records from your physical device.

6. Children's Privacy

Manga Reader is not directed toward or intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.
