Account & Data Deletion

Learn what data is deleted, how to request permanent removal, and how your information is purged from Google Firebase.

Last Updated: November 12, 2026

At Manga Reader, developed by Fays Arukattil, we believe you should have complete ownership and control over your personal data. This document provides clear, step-by-step instructions on how to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated cloud data.

Permanent Action Warning

Deleting your account is permanent. All your synchronized reading history, bookmarks, custom categories, and saved series records in Google Cloud Firestore will be irrecoverably wiped.

1. Data That Will Be Deleted

Upon receiving and verifying your deletion request, we permanently expunge:

  • Firebase Authentication Record: Your Google User Identifier (UID), email address, and display name stored in Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Cloud Firestore Library Documents: All bookmarks, chapter progress, scroll depth records, and custom categories linked to your account.
  • Cached Cloud Metadata: Any temporary session or synchronization tokens.

2. Data That Is Retained

Zero Data Retained: We do not retain any personal or reading data after the deletion request is processed. We do not maintain off-site marketing databases or share data with data brokers.

3. How to Request Account Deletion

You can request account deletion through either of the following methods:

Method 1: Direct Email Request (Fastest)

Send an email from the Google account you used to sign into Manga Reader with the subject Manga Reader Account Deletion Request.

Send Deletion Request Email

Method 2: In-App Local Wipe & Sign-Out

  1. Open Manga Reader on your device.
  2. Tap your profile avatar in the top bar to open the Profile Sheet.
  3. Tap Sign Out to unlink your Google account from the physical device.
  4. Go to your phone's Settings > Apps > Manga Reader > Storage > Clear Storage / Cache to wipe all locally cached comic chapters.

4. Deletion Processing Timeline

Email requests are verified and processed within 7 business days (and no later than 30 days). Once the deletion is completed, you will receive a confirmation email.
